# General Challenge 1

## Challenge No. 1
**Question:**  
How many payment transactions were greater than $5.00?

<details>
  <summary><strong>Hints</strong></summary>

- Use the **payment** table
- Use `COUNT` and `WHERE` with a comparison operator

</details>

---

## Challenge No. 2
**Question:**  
How many actors have a first name that starts with the letter **P**?

<details>
  <summary><strong>Hints</strong></summary>

- Use the **actor** table
- Use `LIKE` with a wildcard such as `%` or `_`

</details>

---

## Challenge No. 3
**Question:**  
How many unique districts are our customers from?

<details>
  <summary><strong>Hints</strong></summary>

- Use `COUNT` and `DISTINCT`

</details>

## Challenge No. 4
**Question:**  
Retrieve the list of names from those distinct districts from the previous question.

<details>

- Use `DISTINCT` (no `COUNT` required)

</details>
